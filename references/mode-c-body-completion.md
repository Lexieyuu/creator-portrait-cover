# Mode C — Body Completion

Use when the supplied person material is mainly a head/face, or visible body information is too limited for a coherent 3:4 cover.

## Rules

- The head is an absolute identity anchor. Preserve face shape, features, expression, hair, age impression, head angle, gaze, and orientation.
- Infer only the minimum needed: neck, shoulders, chest, or a limited upper torso.
- Follow the head's perspective and neck direction. Use a restrained, relaxed, natural posture.
- Continue any visible clothing, collar, jewelry, and color cues; do not invent a costume or a fashion pose.
- Keep the completed body subordinate to the face and consistent with the scene's camera and lighting.

## Do not add by default

Full arms, hands, palms, complex gestures, waist-down body, legs, dramatic twists, exaggerated posture, or a strong new emotion.
