# Photographic Integration and Lifelike Feel

## Photographic Integration

Make all layers share the same camera logic: perspective, scale, horizon, depth of field, grain, sharpness, color response, and motion/static character. Blend edges naturally and preserve realistic contact shadows and occlusion. Avoid a cutout silhouette, uniform blur, plastic skin, excessive HDR, or a perfectly clean synthetic background.

## Lighting Integration

Match key-light direction, softness, color temperature, exposure, contrast, ambient fill, and bounce color. Shadows should describe contact and depth without becoming dramatic unless the source environment calls for it.

## Lifelike creator feel

Favor natural skin texture, small asymmetries, ordinary lived-in spaces, modest imperfections, conversational framing, and a sense of a real person being present. Avoid glossy beauty retouching, fashion-editorial posing, sterile studio backgrounds, hyper-saturated color, uncanny symmetry, and generic AI detail.

## Composition

Use 3:4 vertical by default. Keep the face readable and the person dominant; reserve enough environmental context to make the image feel situated. Do not cover important facial features or hands with decorative text or graphics unless requested.

The default composition is a clean, text-free image layer. Use negative space, walls, windows, shelves, plants, artwork, or quiet architectural areas as future copy space; never fill that space with invented typography, graphic decoration, or a desktop arrangement. Keep the lower foreground free of desks, tables, counters, laptop edges, and office props unless they are explicitly required by the supplied environment and user request.
