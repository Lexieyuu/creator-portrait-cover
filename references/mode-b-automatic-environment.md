# Mode B — Automatic Environment

Use when only a person image is supplied and it already contains enough head, shoulders, upper torso, and any necessary hands or held objects.

## Rules

- Preserve the source person's identity, expression, head pose, body crop, pose, clothing, hands, and held objects exactly as supplied.
- Treat the source as a finished portrait layer, not a prompt for generating a new person. Move, scale, and integrate the complete person; do not extend missing arms, hands, waist, legs, or unseen anatomy.
- Generate only a quiet, lived-in background around the protected person: wall, window, shelf, plant, artwork, soft architectural background, or simple interior depth. Do not use a café, office, studio workspace, or any scene that implies a table.
- Do not introduce any desk, table, tabletop, counter, laptop, notebook, keyboard, pen, mug, desk lamp, office accessory, foreground horizontal surface, or cluster of props.
- Keep the environment visually subordinate, with believable depth and natural imperfections.
- Match background perspective, camera distance, focus falloff, and light direction to the person rather than forcing a cinematic or overly polished look.
- Target a 3:4 vertical output and approximately 75% person height when the original crop permits it.
